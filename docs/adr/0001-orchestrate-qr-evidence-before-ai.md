# Orchestrate QR evidence before AI

The backend, not the language model, will select and run an allowed deterministic QR analyzer and will call AI only after evidence has been finalized. Fixed repository fixtures may be resolved from a sample ID and SHA-256 digest without uploading the image or raw payload; arbitrary raw payload upload remains unavailable while the service uses HTTP. This preserves non-execution and privacy guarantees while preventing AI interpretation of client summaries from being described as independent cloud observation.
